Pre-installation checklist

  • An M.2 2230-2280 PCIe NVMe SSD with an M Key
  • A motherboard or port that supports your M.2 SSD Length and key. key shape will correspond to the sections inside your motherboard socket
  • Including but not limited to Standoff & Screw

Proper techniques & common issues

  • You will need to make sure your hands are clean and dry and computer is turned off before operating inside the machinery.
  • Installation will involve gently easing the ssd into place, not forcing it into the socket.
  • When tightening a screw or standoff, do not overtighten.
  • If SSD is not lying flat after installation, it could because you inserted the screw/standoff incorrectly.

Step by step guide

  1. Hold your SSD, sticker/pins side up with your thumb and index finger.
  2. Turn the SSD at a 45 degree angle, making sure the contact pins are facing the socket on your motherboard.
  3. Gently ease it into the socket, and push with your thumb once inside, turning the SSD within a few degrees up or down as needed. A click sound will confirm that the SSD is inserted correctly.
  4. You can now remove your hand. fasten a standoff ontop of where the half cirlce side of the SSD ends.
  5. Push the ssd down onto standoff so it lies flat, and with your other hand, tighten screw into standoff.
  6. SSD installation is now complete. Follow the steps in reverse if you need to remove the SSD
